# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this repository.

This project documents my personal journey of learning mathematics for Artificial Intelligence from scratch. While the notes reflect my own understanding, I appreciate constructive feedback that helps improve their clarity and accuracy.

## How You Can Contribute

You can contribute by:

- Reporting mistakes or typos.
- Suggesting clearer explanations.
- Recommending useful learning resources.
- Improving formatting or readability.
- Sharing ideas that make the notes more beginner-friendly.

## Before Contributing

Please keep contributions:

- Respectful and constructive.
- Beginner-friendly whenever possible.
- Focused on improving the learning experience.
- Relevant to the topic being discussed.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork this repository.
2. Create a new branch for your changes.
3. Make your improvements.
4. Submit a Pull Request with a clear description of what you changed and why.

## Questions or Suggestions

If you have questions, suggestions, or notice something that could be improved, feel free to open an Issue. I'll do my best to review it as time allows.

## Thank You

Whether you're here to learn, suggest improvements, or simply explore the repository, thank you for being part of the journey.
